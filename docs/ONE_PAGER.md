# ReelMetrics — private catalog audit

## Product

ReelMetrics is a local-first workflow for checking catalog CSV quality, reviewing questionable records, and preparing a cleaned export. The public dashboard uses a bundled movie/TV data snapshot; it does not show current streaming availability.

## Likely buyer

Small media/catalog operations teams preparing a migration, reporting handoff, or catalog refresh who want a privacy-conscious file audit without buying a full data-observability platform. This buyer is unvalidated.

## Current capabilities

- Local CLI profiles CSV files, flags common metadata issues, and writes aggregate reports plus clean/review exports.
- Fixed public web demo shows completeness and a human review queue; decisions stay in the visitor's browser.
- Client files are not uploaded by the current CLI. No team accounts or subscription billing exist.

## Offer to test

A one-off, fixed-scope local audit with a reviewed issue report and cleaned CSV. Agree data handling, scope, turnaround, price, support and deletion terms before receiving any customer file. Do not promise automatic correction or perfect data quality.

## Proof needed before sale

Confirm at least one real buyer and willingness to pay; verify bundled dataset/brand rights; complete a privacy and retention review; test an end-to-end customer file workflow; confirm hosting terms for any commercial demo; define invoice, support and incident boundaries.

## Go/no-go signal

Continue only if a qualified buyer approves a bounded pilot and the measured delivery hours, direct costs, customer outcome and data safeguards make it viable. No customer or revenue is currently verified.
